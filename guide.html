Using the calculator
1

G-code File

Upload your sliced G-code file. When readable metadata is present, PrintProfit can automatically fill in print time and material usage. You can still enter the values manually.

2

Printer Profile

Choose your printer or use a custom profile. The selected profile supplies estimated power draw, purchase price and lifetime figures used for electricity and depreciation.

3

Filament / Resin

Select the material, package size and package cost. For resin printers, the calculator switches the material measurements to millilitres. Usage can come from the file or be entered manually.

4

Operating Costs

Add labour, packaging, other costs and electricity. These are combined with material cost and printer depreciation to estimate the cost of making one print.

5

Selling & Fulfilment

Choose a selling platform and account for platform/payment fees. Add your delivery cost and, where relevant, the amount you charge the customer for delivery.

6

Quantity / Batch Pricing

Set the quantity and an optional batch discount. The results show the production cost, sales, discount, delivery, fees, profit and effective price per item.

Understanding the numbers

Total Cost to Make

The estimated cost of producing the print from the values entered, including the calculator's included material, electricity, depreciation, labour, packaging, other and delivery costs.

Selling Price

The price you enter, or a price generated from one of the target-margin buttons. It is separate from the cost to make.

Profit

The calculator subtracts the included costs and applicable platform/payment fees from the selling amount, with delivery charged handled separately where entered.

Margin

The displayed margin is the profit expressed as a percentage of selling price for the selected result.

Settings explained

Dark mode

Switch between the standard dark PrintProfit interface and a lighter display.

Language

Choose English or Polski. Your choice is saved on the device and applied to the calculator interface where translations are available.

Units

Metric uses grams and millilitres. Imperial switches material entry to ounces and fluid ounces as appropriate.

Currency

Choose GBP, EUR, USD or PLN. The exchange-rate field controls the conversion used for non-GBP display, so it can be updated with your own rate.

Information & help
Why does my result look wrong?

Check print time, material used, package cost, electricity rate, printer profile and platform fees first. Pre-filled values are estimates and may not match your actual setup.

Should I use the automatic G-code values?

Use them when the file contains the metadata PrintProfit can read, then review the numbers against your slicer's estimates before relying on the final price.

What should I put in the electricity rate?

Enter your electricity tariff in £/kWh when using GBP, or the equivalent base-currency rate used by the calculator. Your actual tariff is more useful than a generic estimate.

Why can fees change?

Marketplace, payment and delivery charges depend on the platform, account, product category, programme and service used. Replace the pre-filled assumptions with your real charges whenever possible.

For the most accurate result, use your own material cost, actual material usage, real electricity tariff, actual printer power draw where available, real labour time and the fees you actually pay.
