Your Model & file intelligence

G-code and supported files

PrintProfit recognises G-code and several common 3D-printing formats, including G-code variants, 3MF, Lychee, CHITUBOX, resin slice formats and common model formats such as STL, OBJ, AMF, PLY and STEP/STP. What PrintProfit can calculate automatically depends on what information the file contains.

Automatic print data

Readable sliced data can provide print time and material usage. G-code metadata may supply time, filament grams, filament length or volume. 3MF projects can also expose slicer, printer, profile, nozzle, layer height, infill, supports and bed information where stored.

File analysis status

The Print Information area tells you whether the file analysis is detailed, basic, limited or still waiting. Missing data is not guessed; enter it manually when necessary.

Apply detected settings

When file metadata includes useful printer or material information, use Apply detected settings to transfer the detected values into the calculator. Check the values against your slicer before relying on the final result.

Print Setup

Printer

Select a supported printer profile or choose Custom. A printer profile supplies estimated power and a printer price/lifetime figure used for electricity and depreciation calculations. Printer prices are reference values and can change.

Material type

Choose Filament (FDM) or Resin (SLA / MSLA / DLP). Resin printer profiles can automatically move the calculator into resin mode.

Material cost

Enter package size and package cost, then enter how much material is used per print. PrintProfit calculates the material cost from the amount used rather than charging the whole spool or bottle to one print.

Print time

Use the detected print time where available, or enter it manually. Print time affects electricity and printer depreciation and therefore changes the total cost to make.

Operating Costs

Labour

Enter labour hours per print and your labour rate per hour. This represents your time preparing, finishing, packing or otherwise working on each print.

Packaging & other

Packaging is a per-print cost. Other is for additional costs that do not fit the main fields, such as consumables or small operating expenses.

Electricity

Enter your unit electricity rate (£/kWh), or use an available reference benchmark as a starting point. The calculator combines the printer's estimated power draw with print time to estimate electricity usage and cost.

Actual tariff matters

Supplier names and benchmark rates are reference information only. Your real tariff can differ because of supplier, plan, region, meter type and payment method, so use the rate from your bill whenever possible.

Selling & Fulfilment

Platform fees

Select your selling platform to preload its configured platform fee, payment fee and fixed fee assumptions. You can edit those fields when your own account charges differ.

Delivery

Select a courier and service to load a reference rate. The automatic rate can still be overridden in the Cost to you field when your actual shipping cost is different.

Customer delivery charge

Cost to you is the amount you pay the courier. Charged to customer is the amount you collect from the customer. The calculator keeps these values separate when calculating profit.

Reference rates

Courier and platform figures are estimates/reference data. Parcel size, dimensions, destination, account type, surcharges, promotions and fee changes can alter the final amount.

Quantity & Batch Pricing

Quantity

Set how many identical items you are pricing. Quantity of one represents a single print; a quantity greater than one automatically switches the results view to Batch Pricing.

Batch discount

Enter the percentage discount applied to the selling price per item for the batch. This changes batch sales and therefore batch profit and margin.

Selling price per item

Enter the price you intend to charge for one item. The calculator then uses the quantity, discount, fees and delivery inputs to calculate the batch outcome.

Single vs Batch result

Single Print shows per-print cost, selling price, profit and margin. Batch Pricing shows the cost, sales and profit for the full quantity using the current batch discount and fee assumptions.

Results & target pricing

Total Cost to Make

This combines the applicable material, electricity, printer depreciation, labour, packaging, other costs and delivery cost for the selected result.

Selling Price, Profit & Margin

Selling Price is what you charge. Profit is the amount left after the included costs and applicable selling fees. Margin is profit expressed as a percentage of selling price.

Quick price buttons

The 30%, 50% and 60% buttons set a target margin using the current costs and fees. Clicking an active quick button again can undo it and restore the previous selling price.

Custom target margin

Enter your own target margin, such as 42.5%, to calculate a price from the current cost and fee assumptions. Batch target pricing also uses the current quantity, discount and delivery charge.

Profit Toolkit

What If?

Test changes without permanently changing the main calculation. Explore lower material usage, less print time, labour savings, packaging or delivery savings, or a different selling platform.

Cost Breakdown

See how the current cost to make is divided between material, electricity, printer depreciation, labour, packaging/other costs and delivery. This helps identify which inputs are contributing most to cost.

Price Ladder

See the selling price needed for break-even and a range of target margins using the current selling channel, fixed fee and delivery charge.

Bulk Buy

Compare a bulk material or packaging purchase against your current price and estimate saving per pack, total saving and saving per print.

Sell Where?

Compare fee impact across available selling channels at your current selling price. Platform assumptions may not include every monthly, promotional or optional charge.

Profit status

The Results area gives a quick status such as Profit, Break-even or Loss together with the current margin so you can read the outcome quickly.

My Materials

Save regular materials

My Materials lets you keep reusable filament and resin profiles with a name, type, brand, colour, package amount and cost.

Use a saved material

Select Use on a saved material to apply its details to the calculator instead of entering the same package information repeatedly.

Edit or delete

Saved material profiles can be edited or deleted. They are separate from My Projects.

Currency

Material library values are stored for reuse and displayed using your selected calculator currency and conversion settings.

My Projects & saving

Temporary working draft

PrintProfit keeps a temporary working draft on the current device while you work and when you deliberately move between calculator areas such as Compare Products or Profit Advisor. This prevents ordinary section changes from losing your inputs.

Save Project

Save Project creates a named project containing the current calculator setup. After loading a project, Save becomes Update so changes can be saved back to that same project.

Manage saved projects

My Projects lets you Load, Update, Rename, Duplicate and Delete saved projects. Reset clears the current working state but does not delete separately saved projects.

Where saved projects live

Projects are saved on this device only and are not automatically available on another computer. They are not cloud-synchronised. Keep an exported/backup copy or the original print files separately if you need to move work between devices.

Compare Products

Search & filter

Search product names, retailers, brands, colours, pack/build details or material type. Filter by category, brand, material, colour, retailer, price range and availability.

Smart search & quick filters

Common aliases such as Bambu/Bambu Lab, PLA Plus/PLA+ and FFF/FDM are recognised. Quick filters let you jump to Filament, 3D printers, In stock or Under £20.

Select & compare

Tick the box beside a product and keep selecting while changing filters or brands. The comparison tray keeps your selections for the current browser session.

Product details

Use Details to see the product image, category, brand, material/technology, colour, retailer, pack/build volume, price, unit cost, availability and any recorded price history.

Saved comparisons

Save a selected group as a named comparison. Saved lists are stored locally on this device and can be loaded or deleted later.

Prices, images & freshness

Reference listings show checked dates and a live/reference status. Price history builds as future refreshes record changes. Missing product photos use a safe fallback; the refresh job can discover exact retailer images where supported.

Delivery & final checkout

Delivered-price information is shown only when shipping data is available. Final retailer checkout remains the source of truth for delivery, VAT, coupons, bundles, account pricing and stock.

Check live before buying

Compare Products is a comparison aid, not a guarantee of checkout price. Open the live retailer listing before purchasing.

Settings explained

Dark mode

Switch between the standard dark PrintProfit interface and the lighter display.

Language

Choose from English, Polski, Deutsch, Français, Español, Italiano, Nederlands, Português, Čeština, Svenska or Dansk. The selected language is saved on this device where translated text is available.

Units

Choose metric or imperial measurements. Material measurements change to suit the selected unit system.

Currency

Choose the currency used to display costs and prices. The exchange-rate field controls non-GBP conversion and can be adjusted when you have a more appropriate rate.

Use Apply Changes after changing settings so the calculator refreshes its display and calculations using the selected preferences.

Reset clears the current calculator inputs and temporary working state. It does not delete the separately saved projects or saved materials.

Common questions
Why does my result look wrong?

Check the printer profile, print time, material amount and cost, labour, electricity rate, delivery, selling platform and all applicable fees. Replace reference values with your actual costs wherever possible.

Why did nothing auto-fill from my file?

The file may not contain readable metadata. PrintProfit does not guess missing values. Enter print time and material usage manually, or use a sliced file that includes the metadata needed for analysis.

Why does the file show detailed analysis but the calculator value is different?

File intelligence can report information separately from the live calculator inputs. Review detected values and use Apply detected settings when appropriate, then confirm the final printer, material and usage values.

Why did the result switch to Batch Pricing?

Quantity greater than one automatically selects the Batch Pricing result view. Returning quantity to one selects the single-print result.

Does saving a project save it in the cloud?

No. My Projects are currently local to the device and browser where they were saved. A different computer will not automatically have access to them.

Can I rely on the listed electricity, courier, platform or product prices?

Use them as reference starting points only. Provider tariffs, delivery rates, marketplace fees, product prices, promotions and stock can change. Check the live or actual source whenever accuracy matters.

What is the safest way to price a real order?

Use the actual material cost and usage, real print time, a realistic labour rate, your actual electricity tariff, the real delivery cost, the actual customer delivery charge and the selling platform fees you pay.

Important: PrintProfit is an estimating tool. Provider prices, marketplace fees, delivery charges, electricity rates and other market values may not be current. Estimates should be checked against your actual costs before you use them for a real sale.
