Using the calculator
1

Your Model

Upload your sliced G-code file. When readable metadata is available, PrintProfit can fill in print time and material usage. The live Print Information area shows the file, print time, material used and material detected. Values can still be entered or adjusted manually.

2

Print Setup

Choose your printer and material in one place. Printer profiles provide the figures used for power and depreciation. Resin printer profiles automatically switch the calculator to resin measurements; custom printers let you choose the material type yourself.

3

Costs & Fees

Enter operating costs, selling and fulfilment details, then set quantity and any batch discount. When quantity is greater than one, PrintProfit automatically switches the results view to Batch Pricing.

4

Results

Review the estimated cost to make, selling price, fees, profit and margin. Target-pricing options can calculate a selling price from the margin you want to achieve.

Your PrintProfit libraries

My Projects

Save a complete calculator setup, including your entered values. Saved projects can be loaded, duplicated or deleted from the My Projects area.

My Materials

Keep reusable filament and resin profiles with brand, colour, package size and cost. Using a saved material can populate the calculator for you, and displayed costs follow your selected currency.

Profit Toolkit

What If?

Test possible changes without altering your live calculation. Try reducing material usage or print time, saving labour minutes, lowering packaging or delivery costs, or comparing a different selling platform. The tool shows the adjusted profit and cost as an estimate.

Cost Breakdown

Visual bars show how much of the current cost to make comes from material, electricity, printer depreciation, labour, packaging and other costs, delivery, and platform/payment fees.

Price Ladder

See the selling price needed for break-even and for 10%, 20%, 30%, 40% and 50% margins using the current selling channel, fees and delivery charge.

Bulk Buy

Enter a bulk material or packaging price to estimate saving per pack, total buying saving and saving per print. Use your real supplier prices rather than assumptions.

Sell Where?

Compare the fee impact of the available selling channels at your current selling price. The figures use the fee assumptions configured in PrintProfit; monthly plans, promotions and optional charges may not be included.

Profit status

The Results heading shows a quick calculated status for Profit, Break-even or Loss together with the current margin, so you can understand the result at a glance.

Understanding the numbers

Total Cost to Make

The estimated cost of producing the print from the values entered, including the calculator's material, electricity, depreciation, labour, packaging, other and applicable delivery costs.

Selling Price

The price you enter, or a price generated from a target-margin option. It is separate from the cost to make.

Profit

The calculator subtracts the included production costs and applicable platform/payment fees from the selling amount. Delivery charged to the customer is handled separately where entered.

Margin

The displayed margin expresses profit as a percentage of selling price for the selected result.

Settings explained

Dark mode

Switch between the standard dark PrintProfit interface and the lighter display.

Language

Choose from English, Polski, Deutsch, Français, Español, Italiano, Nederlands, Português, Čeština, Svenska or Dansk. The selected language is saved on the device and applied to the interface where translated text is available.

Units

Metric uses grams and millilitres where applicable. Imperial switches material measurements to ounces and fluid ounces as appropriate.

Currency

Choose from the currencies provided in Settings. The exchange-rate field controls the conversion used for non-GBP display and can be adjusted with your own rate.

Reset Calculator

Reset clears the calculator's current inputs and returns pricing fields to their starting values.

Information & help
Why does my result look wrong?

Check print time, material used, package cost, electricity rate, printer profile, labour, delivery and platform fees. Pre-filled values are estimates and may not match your actual setup.

What happens when I upload a G-code file?

If readable metadata is present, PrintProfit can extract available print information. Always compare the detected values with your slicer's figures before relying on the final price.

Why did the calculator switch to Batch Pricing?

Quantity greater than one automatically switches the results view to Batch Pricing. Returning the quantity to one switches the view back to the single-print result.

Why did my fields disappear after opening Guide & Help?

Your current calculator setup is now saved automatically on the device while you work. Returning to the calculator restores the saved fields and, where the browser permits it, the last uploaded print file. Reset intentionally clears the saved draft.

Why did my material change to resin?

Supported resin printer profiles automatically select resin mode. This changes the material workflow and measurements to suit resin. Custom printer profiles keep the material-type choice available.

What should I put in the electricity rate?

Enter your actual electricity tariff in the currency shown by the calculator. Your real tariff is more useful than a generic estimate. Printer power and print time also affect the calculated electricity cost.

Why can fees and delivery change the result?

Marketplace, payment and delivery charges depend on the platform, account, product and service used. Replace assumptions with your actual charges whenever possible.

For the most accurate result, use your actual material cost, actual material usage, real electricity tariff, measured or published printer power where available, real labour time and the fees you actually pay.
