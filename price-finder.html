How PrintProfit compares prices

Material comparison

Filament is normalised to a comparable price per kilogram when the package weight is known. This makes different pack sizes easier to compare without treating a 500g or 750g spool as if it were a full kilogram.

Printer comparison

Printers are compared by headline price and shown with build volume where available. Unit-cost sorting is only meaningful for materials, not complete machines.

Source & freshness

Each listing carries a checked date. Prices are reference snapshots, and the live retailer page remains the source of truth for the final checkout price.
