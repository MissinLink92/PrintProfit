How PrintProfit compares prices

Filament: £ / kg

Filament listings are normalised to a per-kilogram figure where the pack weight is known. That makes a 500g spool, 1kg spool and larger bundle easier to compare.

Printers: headline price

Printer listings use the advertised machine price. Build volume and printer type are shown where known so a low price is not mistaken for an identical machine.

Live-check before buying

The comparison is a snapshot. Delivery, coupons, bundles, account pricing, VAT treatment and stock can alter the final checkout price, so use the retailer link before purchasing.
